We introduce curvature as the failure of second covariant derivatives to commute, develop the curvature tensor's symmetries, and define sectional curvature and its traces (Ricci and scalar), with enough computations to make the definitions feel tangible.

\section*{Reading}
From \textit{Riemannian Geometry} by Sylvestre Gallot, Dominique Hulin, and Jacques Lafontaine, read \textsection III.A The Curvature Tensor: ``Second covariant derivative,'' ``Algebraic properties of the curvature tensor,'' ``Computation of curvature: some examples,'' and ``Ricci curvature, scalar curvature.''
