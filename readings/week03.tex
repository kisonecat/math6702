We introduce covariant differentiation as the tool that replaces ordinary derivatives on curved spaces, and we isolate the Levi-Civita connection as the unique connection compatible with the metric and torsion-free, then begin parallel transport.

\section*{Reading}
From \textit{Riemannian Geometry} by Sylvestre Gallot, Dominique Hulin, and Jacques Lafontaine, read \textsection II.B Covariant Derivative: ``Connections,'' ``Canonical connection of a Riemannian submanifold,'' ``Extension of the covariant derivative to tensors,'' ``Covariant derivative along a curve,'' ``Parallel transport,'' and ``Examples.''
