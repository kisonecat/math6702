We use curvature bounds to extract topological consequences, especially for the fundamental group in negative curvature and for pinched curvature, and we situate Preissmann-type conclusions and Berger-type sphere theorems within the pinching framework presented in the text.

\section*{Reading}
From \textit{Riemannian Geometry} by Sylvestre Gallot, Dominique Hulin, and Jacques Lafontaine, read \textsection III.I Curvature and Growth of the Fundamental Group: ``Growth of finite type groups'' and ``Growth of the fundamental group of compact manifolds with negative curvature,'' together with \textsection III.J Curvature and Topology: An Account of Some Old and Recent Results: ``Introduction,'' ``Traditional point of view: pinched manifolds,'' ``Almost flat pinching,'' and ``Coarse point of view: compactness theorems of Cheeger and Gromov,'' and, as needed for curvature identities used in pinching arguments, \textsection III.K Curvature Tensors and Representations of the Orthogonal Group: ``The second Bianchi identity.''
