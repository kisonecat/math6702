We develop Jacobi fields as the linearized geodesic equation, relate them to the exponential map and conjugate points, and use the index form viewpoint to set up and prove Rauch-type comparison statements in the contexts we will use later.

\section*{Reading}
From \textit{Riemannian Geometry} by Sylvestre Gallot, Dominique Hulin, and Jacques Lafontaine, read \textsection III.C Jacobi Vector Fields: ``Basic topics about second derivatives,'' ``Index form,'' ``Jacobi fields and exponential map,'' and ``Applications: $S^n$, $H^n$, $\mathbf{P}^n_{\mathbb{R}}$, 2-dimensional Riemannian manifolds.''
