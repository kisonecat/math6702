We build a working repertoire of standard Riemannian constructions (submanifolds, products, coverings, and submersions) so that later theorems have concrete test cases.

\section*{Reading}
From \textit{Riemannian Geometry} by Sylvestre Gallot, Dominique Hulin, and Jacques Lafontaine, read \textsection II.A Existence Theorems and First Examples: ``Examples: Riemannian submanifolds, product Riemannian manifolds,'' ``Riemannian covering maps, flat tori,'' ``Riemannian submersions, complex projective space,'' and ``Homogeneous Riemannian spaces.''
