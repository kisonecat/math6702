We finish with submanifold geometry: second fundamental form and curvature computations, and then use convexity results to reach the Hadamard theorem as a geometric capstone, ending with an integrated review of the course's main themes.

\section*{Reading}
From \textit{Riemannian Geometry} by Sylvestre Gallot, Dominique Hulin, and Jacques Lafontaine, read \textsection V.A Curvature of Submanifolds: ``Introduction,'' ``Second fundamental form,'' ``Curvature of hypersurfaces,'' and ``Application to explicit computations of curvatures,'' together with \textsection V.B Curvature and Convexity: ``The Hadamard theorem.''
